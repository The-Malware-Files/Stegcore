features:
  - title: Nothing leaves your machine
    details: No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry, no network calls of any kind. The engine is one native binary with no runtime to install first.
  - title: Two messages, two passphrases
    details: Deniable mode carries a decoy alongside the real message. Neither half is marked, and which one holds what is decided by a coin flip at embedding time.
  - title: It detects as well as it hides
    details: Three detectors match the Aletheia reference to floating-point precision, with thresholds fitted against real photographs at a stated false-alarm rate rather than guessed.
  - title: Honest about its limits
    details: Strong on spatial LSB and on tools that leave a signature. Weak on very small payloads and LSB matching. JPEG DCT hiding is not covered, and the docs say so.
